# Third-party materials

This distribution combines a knowledge snapshot, referenced source code and archived experimental evidence. Packaging does not replace or broaden the licenses of the included materials.

- The pinned Newton source tree includes its Apache License 2.0 at `knowledge-base/sources/newton-v1.7.0/LICENSE.md`. Preserve its license, notices and individual source headers.
- Mermaid and KaTeX retain their bundled license files at `knowledge-base/site/dist/vendor/mermaid/LICENSE` and `knowledge-base/site/dist/vendor/katex/LICENSE`.
- Python dependencies are installed separately from `requirements.lock.txt` and retain their own licenses and package metadata.
- Research papers remain subject to their authors' and publishers' rights. The default release includes citations, mappings and source metadata, with original paper assets omitted. Including paper originals in a custom export does not grant additional redistribution rights.

No new blanket license for the original knowledge-base content is granted by this packaging change. The maintainer can select an appropriate project license separately before presenting the repository as an open-source project.
